# Sense2Sim-VECTOR

VECTOR: Velocity Based Temperature-field Monitoring with Distributed Acoustic Devices, as an interactive page made by Sense2Sim.

Online: https://ambient-intelligence-lab-umich-eecs.github.io/Sense2Sim-VECTOR/

- `index.html`: the page; its heavy parts beside it (`library/`, `media/`, `kg/`, `world/`), and the paper's pages (`paper/`) where given.
- The camera's hand and body tracking (MediaPipe) load from their CDN when the reader turns the camera on.
- `CREDITS.md`: the credit and licence of each 3D model.
